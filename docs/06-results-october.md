**Hidden fixed cost is the largest leak.** Of ₹700,000 in monthly fixed costs, production absorbed only ₹163,176 (23%) at the machine-hour rates. Either the standard times in the BOMs are optimistic, or most paid hours don't produce. Both are worth knowing, and the system makes the gap measurable every month. (The standard times here are estimates; with real time studies this number becomes a utilisation KPI.)

## Make or buy: in-house cold press vs bought UV door board

| BOM | Route | Wardrobes made | Actual cost per wardrobe |

| BOM-WRD-3D-001 | Buy UV door board | 4 | ₹20,197 |
| BOM-WRD-3D-002 | Press door board in-house | 8 | ₹20,087 |

Pressing in-house saves **₹110 per 3-door wardrobe (0.5%)** at the estimated prices: marginal. The case for the press is shorter lead time and finish choice, not cost. This matches the doubt raised in discovery, and with real purchase prices the same report settles it.
