# 10. Owner update (plain language)

*One-page status note for the factory owner after the first month on the system. No jargon; every number comes from
the system's own books.*

---

**To:** Owner, Kaveri Furniture Works · **Re:** October on the new system, and what to do next

**1. What we now know that we didn't before**
- We sold **₹30 L** in October (with GST). After *every* cost (materials, salaries, rent, power, damages, discounts)
  the business kept about **₹6.5 L**, roughly **25 paise of every rupee** of sales (before GST).
- The "80–90% margin" is real on the *making cost* of each piece. But only about a quarter of the ₹7 L we pay every
  month in salaries, rent and power shows up in that making cost. The rest is time the factory is paid for but isn't
  producing. That gap, not the price, is where most of the money goes.

**2. Money we lost that can be stopped**

| What happened | Cost in October | Fix already set up |
|---|---|---|
| Sheets ran out, so workers sat idle for about 1½ days | ₹41,000 | The system now tells us to reorder sheets at 159 in stock, before we run out, instead of waiting for retailer payments |
| Bought sheets in a hurry from Chennai at 8% extra | ₹12,000 | Same fix |
| Furniture damaged on the way; we gave free replacements | ₹29,000 | Every damage is logged with its cause. Fix the most common cause first (chipping in transit → better packing) |
| CNC broke down (repair plus a half day lost) | ₹23,000 | Logged; track how often it happens to decide on a service contract |
| Fast-paying retailers took early-payment discounts | ₹16,000 | Worth it for cash flow, but now we can see the cost |
| Hardware missing at the month-end count | ₹2,500 | Monthly count continues; hardware kept in its own store |

Testing the new reorder rule on the same October: **no idle days, ₹50,000 a month back**, and no extra lorry trips.

**3. The cold press**
On our current prices, pressing door boards ourselves saves about **₹110 per wardrobe**, so it doesn't really save
money. Its real value is faster supply and choosing our own finishes. With our actual purchase bills, the system
will confirm this every month.

**4. Custom walk-in wardrobes**
A custom order **can't be confirmed** in the system until it has the measurements, finish, inside layout, a drawing
and the **customer's signature**. If a customer later says "this is not what I asked", we show the signed version.
No more arguments between the shop staff and our staff.

**5. Retailer staff orders**
Our sales staff at the big retailer can now send orders from their phones directly into the system. No re-typing,
nothing lost, no double orders, even if the internet drops.

**What I need from you this week**
1. Our real purchase bills for October (sheets, laminates, hardware), so the costs become exact.
2. A yes on the new reorder rule for sheets.
3. 45 minutes with the accountant, three times, for training on their own entries.
